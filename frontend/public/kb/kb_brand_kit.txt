BrewBase is BrewContent's brand truth layer: "Brand truth: voice, style, and RAG." BrewBase ingests the brand's past landing pages, ads, product docs and video transcripts into a retrieval layer that holds the voice fingerprint (vocabulary, cadence, banned phrases and tone), product knowledge, customer vocabulary, visual identity, and legal and compliance context. The Brand Kit and Brand Voice features sit on top of this: the Free plan includes 1 Brand Kit and 1 Brand Voice. (Source: brewcontent.ai/products/studio and pricing page)

Brand truth setup: connect your CMS and ad accounts and add your best-performing assets. BrewBase ingests them overnight, and most brands have a working voice model within 24 hours. (Source: Studio page FAQ)

BrewContent does not fine-tune models on your brand data. Grounding is done through retrieval (RAG on brand truth) plus adapter layers, never fine-tuning, so new frontier models are available the day they ship. (Source: Studio page FAQ)

Drafts review flow: every generated asset lands in Drafts for human review, where you can approve it, edit it, or send it back. Accepted and edited versions feed Brewloop, the closed-loop learning system that sharpens future generations. (Source: Studio page FAQ)

BrewGuard is BrewContent's brand safety and compliance layer, built in rather than bolted on. BrewGuard checks intellectual property, trademark, brand safety and vertical compliance (including FINRA, HIPAA, FDA and GDPR) inline, before content reaches the review queue, and model provenance is disclosed per asset. (Source: brewcontent.ai homepage Product menu and Studio page FAQ)
