BrewContent Studio is the generation platform: "Text, image, and video grounded in your brand truth." Text outputs include landing pages, blog posts, emails, ads, product descriptions, sales sequences, LinkedIn posts, social captions, SEO briefs and long-form articles. Image outputs include ad creative, hero images, product photography, lifestyle shots, illustrations, social visuals and thumbnails. Video outputs include UGC-style ads, product demos, avatar explainers, story sequences and cinematic brand films. (Source: brewcontent.ai/products/studio)

Motion Suite is BrewContent's video and motion platform: "Video, ads, avatars, and motion at brand quality." Motion Suite has four systems. Still Room: cinematic direction and standing sets. Cask ID: consistent characters, mascots and avatars across content. Proofing Room: performance and dialogue. Bottling Line: cutdowns, aspect ratios and platform variants. Together, the four Motion Suite systems cover the full video workflow from direction to platform-ready cutdowns. (Source: brewcontent.ai homepage Product menu and Studio page)

Studio creative capabilities include per-surface voice adapters: eight community-native voices, because the TikTok voice differs from the LinkedIn voice, the Reddit voice and the email voice. Studio also offers moment-based creative from a trigger library, cohort-relevant and retrieval-conditioned generation, and conversation-aware content. Metrics claimed on the Studio page: 10x more assets shipped per week, 89% first-draft acceptance rate, 4.7x faster brief-to-ship, and 73% lower cost per asset versus agency production.

Video generation facts from the pricing FAQ: each video can be 1 to 15 seconds long, depending on the model and quality tier, story complexity and available credits. Video generation is available on all tiers: Free includes 1 video per day, Basic and Pro are limited by creative credits, and Motion is optimised for high-volume video. The Motion tier is designed for storytelling workflows such as brand films, product demos, social reels and multi-scene sequences, and its continuous story building extends scenes across clips while maintaining character and brand consistency.
