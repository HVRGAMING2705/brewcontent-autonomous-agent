BrewContent pricing: how much each plan costs. BrewContent plan prices are per seat per month (Source: brewcontent.ai/pricing). Free: $0. Basic: $29 per seat/month. Pro: $59 per seat/month. Motion: $199 per seat/month. Annual billing saves 10%: the yearly per-seat monthly rates, billed once per year, are Basic $26, Pro $53 and Motion $179. Monthly billing has no commitment, and each seat gets the full tier allocation.

Credit allocations per plan. Free: 2 images per day and 1 video per day of Brew Creative Credits, 30K Text Credits, and 3 social posts per day. Basic: 85 Brew Creative Credits and 140K Text Credits per month, with unlimited post creation. Pro: 172 Brew Creative Credits and 280K Text Credits per month. Motion: 638 Brew Creative Credits and 140K Text Credits per month. Note, as shown on the pricing page: Motion's 140K Text Credits are lower than Pro's 280K Text Credits, even though Motion is the higher-priced plan; Motion's strength is its much larger Creative Credit allocation for video and motion work.

What each plan includes. Free ("Try Brewcontent with daily creative limits"): 1 Brand Kit and 1 Brand Voice, the Job Description generator, the Billing Calculator, premium features limited to 1 whitepaper or 1 case study or 1 blog article, and 2 target audiences. Basic ("Everything in Free, plus"): blogs, case studies and ad copy, email campaigns, Mic Drop audio-to-content, Post Analysis and Content Analysis, and up to 5 target audiences. Pro ("Everything in Basic, plus"): Campaign Canvas, all premium marketing features, Tubemind and advanced ad tools, unlimited target audiences, and priority support. Motion: video and motion graphics creation, all exclusive plan features, continuous story building, enterprise-grade video output, and Campaign Canvas; it is recommended for storytelling workflows, and its premium features (white papers, case studies, blog articles) are shown as "NA" in the comparison table.

Credit rules from the pricing FAQ. Brew Creative Credits are used for image and video generation; higher quality costs more, and consumption depends on output quality and resolution, video length and complexity, number of variations, and model tier. Text Credits are used for written content; typical usage is about 2,000 to 4,000 credits for 1 blog article, 500 to 1,500 for 1 email campaign, and 200 to 500 for 1 social post. Search Credits: 1 Search Credit equals 1 lookup, spent when output is enhanced with real-time intelligence such as trend or competitor research, topic validation, and SEO and LLM optimisation signals.

Add-on credits, available on paid tiers, stack with the monthly allocation: 240K Text Credits for $5, and 125 Brew Creative Credits for $30. On the Free plan, add-ons are shown as "Upgrade to Basic". Plan changes: upgrades apply the new credits immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing cycle; unused credits do not roll over between tiers.

Enterprise plan: custom pricing for multi-brand deployments, SSO, SCIM, compliance, dedicated support and onboarding, arranged through Contact Sales. Examples given in the pricing FAQ: a global mid-market company at $200K to $600K per year, and a Fortune 1000 company at $600K to $2M per year, with volume discounts available.

Feature availability note, recorded exactly as shown on brewcontent.ai/pricing: the comparison table shows Mic Drop, Post Analysis and Content Analysis as included on the Basic, Pro and Motion plans. Some FAQ answers on the same page describe Mic Drop, Search Credits and Post Analysis as available or included in "Basic and Enterprise" plans. Both wordings appear on the pricing page; this knowledge base records both and does not resolve the difference.

Support and contact. The Contact page at brewcontent.ai/contact is a Contact Sales form only, aimed at enterprise pricing, SSO, SCIM, multi-brand and volume plans; no support email address or phone number is displayed on the Contact page or in the site footer. The only email address published on the public site is info@brewcontent.ai. Pro includes priority support, and Enterprise includes dedicated support and onboarding. The Privacy Policy states that support and communications can arrive via support tickets, in-app chat, email or feedback forms.

Cancellation, from the Terms of Service: a subscription cancellation must be notified to info@brewcontent.ai at least 3 days before renewal, and access continues to the end of the billing period.
