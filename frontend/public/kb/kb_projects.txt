Share of Model is BrewContent's headline metric: how often AI recommends your brand, measured across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews. BrewContent positions Share of Model as the replacement for Share of Voice, because a brand's first audience is increasingly the AI agent answering the buyer. OmniBrew is the component named for Share of Model measurement, and the AI Search pillar ("Show up in AI answers") is built around it. (Source: brewcontent.ai homepage and the Autonomous Demand OS product page)
